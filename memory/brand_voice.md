## Banned phrases

- "100% waterproof" → use "waterproof for daily wear" (legally safer)
- "Shop now! Limited stock!" → too pushy, off-brand
- "DM for price" → never; link to website (prices change)
- Exact rupee amounts in IG DMs → link to product page instead
- "Sister", "babe", "love" as openers in cold DMs → too forward

## Channel modulation

| Channel | Voice |
|---|---|
| Instagram captions / Reels | Full Hindi-English voice (Saarit) |
| Cold outreach DMs | Lighter Hindi-English; warmer; no slang the recipient might not get |
| Customer service DMs | Friendly, professional, mostly English with light Hindi |
| Email (transactional) | Cleaner English, friendly |
| Email (marketing) | Brand voice, Hindi-English ok |
