# Price rules

## Hard rules (no exceptions without escalation)

1. **Never quote exact prices in IG DMs.** Always link to the product page on www.houseofyeashi.in. Prices change; a DM with a wrong price is a customer-service nightmare.
2. **Never offer discounts greater than ₹100** without explicit Vanshika approval.
3. **Never promise free shipping** unless Shopify already shows it for that order.
4. **Never promise delivery dates.** Direct customers to the Shopify order tracking link instead.

## Pricing context for agents

- **Range:** ₹200 – ₹2,000 across the catalog
- **Current AOV:** under ₹500 — increasing this is a brand goal
- **Mystery Box tiers:** ₹999 / ₹1,999 / ₹2,999

## Tone for price questions

When a DM asks "kitne ka hai?" / "what's the price?":
- Friendly redirect to the product page
- Acknowledge the question (don't just paste a link)
- Offer to help find the right piece if they share what they liked

Sample: *"Hii! Ye [Dilruba] ka link bhej rahi hu — yahan saare details + price hai 🔗 [link]. Koi aur necklace pasand aaya toh batao!"* (Marketing Head will refine this in the actual brand voice guide.)

## When prices change

If Vanshika updates a price on Shopify:
- Web Developer agent gets a notification
- Marketing Head pauses any in-flight email mentioning the old price until reviewed
